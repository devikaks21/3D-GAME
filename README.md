# OPEN ROAD 3D — Open-World Driving Simulator

A feature-rich, high-performance 3D driving simulator built with HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and Three.js (WebGL).

---

## 🚀 Quick Start (Running Locally from VS Code)

### Option 1: Built-in Node Server (Recommended)
1. Open the project folder in **VS Code**.
2. Open the terminal (`Ctrl + ~`) and run:
   ```bash
   npm start
   ```
3. Open your browser and navigate to:
   ```
   http://localhost:3000
   ```

### Option 2: VS Code Live Server
1. Install the **Live Server** extension in VS Code.
2. Right-click `index.html` and select **"Open with Live Server"**.

## 🗺️ Connected Open-World Map (14 Locations & Features)

The entire world is interconnected seamlessly via the multi-lane **Ring Road Highway** and arterial city avenues:

1. **CITY**:
   - Main multi-lane avenues and side streets with marked traffic lanes.
   - Street intersections with cycling traffic signals (Green, Yellow, Red) and street lamps.
   - High-rise commercial skyscrapers, street shops with awnings and storefront glass.
   - Street parking bays along sidewalks.

2. **PARK**:
   - Emerald Public Park with open green lawns.
   - Winding walking paths and central cobblestone plaza.
   - Central water fountain with stone spire.
   - Park benches, trees, and perimeter stone hedges.

3. **CAR DEALERSHIP (Showroom)**:
   - Modern luxury glass pavilion showroom ("APEX MOTOR SHOWROOM").
   - Rotating illuminated display turntables with real 3D display vehicles.
   - Customer parking bays.
   - Interactive Showroom Entrance: Drive up to the showroom and press **[E]** to open the vehicle showroom selector.

4. **GARAGE (Tuning Facility)**:
   - Physical drive-in workshop bay with hydraulic service lift.
   - Tool chests, tire racks, and overhead fluorescent lighting.
   - Drive-in interaction: Press **[E]** inside the bay to customize paint, rims, calipers, and neon underglow.

5. **CAR WASH**:
   - Express automated drive-through wash tunnel.
   - Rotating blue foam brush rollers (overhead and dual side rollers).
   - Water spray arches and cascading water/foam particles.
   - Drive through to trigger automatic cleaning sequence and restore vehicle gleam!

6. **PETROL STATION**:
   - Octane 24/7 service station with large illuminated overhead canopy.
   - 4 Dual-sided fuel pump islands with digital screens.
   - "OPEN ROAD MART" convenience store with parking spaces.
   - Interactive Refuel: Pull up to any pump and press **[F]** to refill the fuel tank to 100%!

7. **AIRPORT**:
   - 750-meter paved runway for high-speed drag runs and top-speed testing.
   - Runway threshold piano keys, centerline stripes, and cyan runway edge lights.
   - Passenger terminal building, glass concourse, and airport parking lot.
   - Air traffic control tower and aircraft hangars.

8. **BEACH**:
   - 700-meter sandy coastal shoreline with animated ocean waves.
   - Coastal scenic drive boulevard connecting to the city and highway.
   - Tropical palm tree promenade, wooden pier with pylons, and coastal lighthouse.

9. **SCHOOL**:
   - Oakridge Academy brick school campus with central clock tower.
   - Classroom wings, classical pillars, entrance steps, and school signs.
   - School drop-off loop, zebra crosswalks, and 30 km/h School Zone speed limit signs.

10. **APARTMENTS**:
    - Skyline residential apartment towers (9 to 12 floors).
    - Multi-floor architecture with individual balconies and glass railings.
    - Ground-floor glass entrance lobbies and resident parking bays.

11. **TRAM LINE**:
    - Dual steel grooved tram rails embedded directly into the central avenue asphalt.
    - Overhead electric catenary wires and metallic support masts.
    - Central tram station passenger shelter platform.
    - Active animated electric tram car cruising steadily along the tracks!

12. **RIVERBED**:
    - Natural sunken river channel dividing the downtown metropolis from the alpine mountains.
    - Flowing animated water surface with sloping earthen riverbanks and shoreline rocks.
    - 3 Multi-lane roadway suspension bridges spanning across the riverbed.

13. **PARKING LOT (Practice Area)**:
    - Vast 100m x 90m dedicated driving practice facility.
    - Marked double-sided parking stalls, median curbs, and solar lampposts.
    - Coned slalom course and tight parallel-parking bays for precision maneuvers.

14. **RING ROAD HIGHWAY**:
    - 4-lane arterial orbital highway enclosing the metropolitan area.
    - Concrete central median barriers dividing highway traffic.
    - Overhead highway directional sign gantries directing traffic to Downtown, Airport, Beach, Mountain, and Circuit!

## 🎮 Driving Controls Reference

| Control | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **W / Up Arrow** | Accelerate / Throttle |
| **S / Down Arrow** | Foot Brake / Reverse |
| **A / Left Arrow** | Steer Left |
| **D / Right Arrow** | Steer Right |
| **SPACE** | Handbrake / Drift Turn |
| **C** | Cycle Camera (Chase, Cockpit, Hood, Bumper, Free Orbit) |
| **L** | Toggle Dynamic Headlights |
| **Q / E** | Left / Right Turn Indicators |
| **H** | Hazard Warning Blinkers |
| **X** | Windshield Wipers |
| **T** | Convertible Folding Roof (Open / Close on Venom Spyder) |
| **G** | Open / Close Doors |
| **K** | Open / Close Boot / Trunk |
| **E** | Enter Showroom / Enter Tuning Garage (when nearby) |
| **F** | Refuel Vehicle at Petrol Station Pump |
| **B** | Dual-Tone Automotive Horn |
| **R** | Reset Vehicle Position & Orientation |
| **ESC** | Pause Menu / Resume |
| **Shift / Ctrl** | Manual Gear Upshift / Downshift (in Manual Transmission mode) |
